## 1. "FAMILYSYNC" -- EXISTING USE ASSESSMENT

### VERDICT: HIGH RISK -- Name is already in active use

**FamilySync by Software MacKiev (Genealogy Software)**
- "FamilySync" is an existing feature and registered trademark of **The Software MacKiev Company**.
- It is the syncing technology used in **Family Tree Maker** (genealogy software) to sync family trees with Ancestry.com.
- FamilySync replaced Ancestry's older "TreeSync" feature in 2017.
- The FamilySync name and logo are **registered trademarks**.
- Source: [Ancestry and Software MacKiev Introduce FamilySync](https://www.familytree.com/blog/ancestry-and-software-mackiev-introduce-familysync/)

**FamilySync LLC (App Developer)**
- There is a separate entity called **FamilySync LLC** registered as a developer on the Apple App Store.
- They publish an app called "Flexical."
- Source: [FamilySync LLC on App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/developer/familysync-llc/id1842488693)

### Trademark Risk
While a direct USPTO search did not surface a public filing in web results, the fact that Software MacKiev states "FamilySync and the FamilySync logo are registered trademarks" means there is a strong likelihood of an existing trademark. A formal TESS search at [tmsearch.uspto.gov](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) is recommended to confirm.

## 5. SUMMARY & RECOMMENDATION

### Do NOT proceed with "FamilySync"
- It is a **registered trademark** of Software MacKiev (genealogy software).
- A separate **FamilySync LLC** also exists as an app publisher.
- **All four domain variants** (.com, .app, .io, getfamilysync.com) are taken.
- Risk of trademark infringement and customer confusion is high.

1. **Secure domains immediately** for your top 1-2 choices (domains can be grabbed quickly).
